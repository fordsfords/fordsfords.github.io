The Jar

I have a jar I cannot open
Though I twist and turn with all my might.
I grunt and groan, swear and fight,
But still the lid stays fastened tight.

This cursed jar does vex me so -
Its contents close but still forbidden.
A birthday gift, so kindly given;
For three long years I have striven.

I think that some day I'll succeed,
And retain a bit of manly pride,
And vindicate the tears I've cried,
And finally discover what's inside.
